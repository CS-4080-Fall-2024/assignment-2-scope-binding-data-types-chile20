Question 1: A = B * (C * (A + B))
Grammar:
    Statement → Assignment | Expression
    Assignment → Identifier = Expression
    Expression → Expression + Term | Term
    Term → Term * Factor | Factor
    Factor → ( Expression ) | Identifier
    Identifier → A | B | C
---
Derivation:
    Statement → Assignment
    Assignment → Identifier = Expression:  A = B * (C * (A + B))
    Identifier → A
    Expression → Term: B * (C * (A + B))
    Term → Term * Factor
    Term → Factor → Identifier → B
    Factor → Expression: C * (A + B)
    Expression → Term
    Term → Term * Factor
    Term → Factor → Identifier → C
    Factor → Expression
    Expression → Expression + Term: A + B
    Expression → Term → Factor → Identifier → A
    Term → Factor → Identifier → B
---

Question 2:
a. Using static scope, variables always refer to its top level environment.
So we will ignore any additional assignment of u, v, w in the functions.
______________________________________
| add(v)                              |
______________________________________
| bar (add)   |  u = 42 v = 69 w = 17 |
______________________________________
| foo (u, 13) |  u = 42 v = 69 w = 17 |
_______________________________________
| main        |  u = 42 v = 69 w = 17 |

    Global: u = 42; v = 69; w = 17
        Foo(x = u, w): local v = u = 42
            Bar(Add): local u = w = 17
                Add(z = v)
                    u = v + u + z
                    with v = 69, u = 42, z = v = 69 (global values)
    So u = 69 + 42+ 69 = 180
b. Using dynamic scope with deep binding, variables refer to the environment at the time
procedure is passed as an argument
______________________________________
| add(v)                              |
______________________________________
| bar (add)   |  u = 42 v = 42 w = 17 |
______________________________________
| foo (u, 13) |  u = 42 v = 42 w = 17 |
_______________________________________
| main        |  u = 42 v = 69 w = 17 |
    Global: u = 42; v = 69; w = 17
        Foo(x = u, w): local v = u = 42
            Bar(Add): local u = w = 17
                Add(z = v)
                    u = v + u + z
                    with v = 42, z = v = 42 from Foo environment since it was where Add was passed as an argument
                    with u = 42 from global scope since u was not declared in Foo
    So u = 42 + 42 + 42 = 126

c. Using dynamic scope with shallow binding, variables refer to the environment at the
time procedure is actually called
______________________________________
| add(v)                              |
______________________________________
| bar (add)   |  u = 17 v = 42 w = 17 |
______________________________________
| foo (u, 13) |  u = 42 v = 42 w = 17 |
_______________________________________
| main        |  u = 42 v = 69 w = 17 |
    Global: u = 42; v = 69; w = 17
        Foo(x = u, w): local v = u = 42
            Bar(Add): local u = w = 17
                Add(z = v)
                    u = v + u + z
                    with v = 42, z = v = 42 from environment Bar. Since v is not re-assigned in Bar,
                    local v in Bar = 42 (from environment Foo where Bar was called)
                    with u = 17 from environment Bar where Add was called

    So u = 42 + 17 + 42 = 101
